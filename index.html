Character Traits

An Edge is an advantage your character has based on something in their past. Depending on the situation, the player can argue to the GM why the Edge's mechanical bonus applies. Example: Parrien was raised by his farmer father. +1d12 when doing anything a farmhand would know how to do, such as tying ropes, riding horses, handling animals, or using farm tools as weapons.
A weakness from your character's past that imposes a situational mechanical disadvantage. Example: Parrien's father was attacked by a knight wielding a warhammer and badly injured. -3 Evasion against attacks with warhammers.
A long-term roleplaying goal, not a mechanical modifier. Acting toward it can earn a special boon during a long rest. Example: Parrien wants to become a knight so he can find his father's attacker and bring him to justice. After an in-game day in which he meaningfully advances either goal, the player can earn a boon during a long rest.
A recurring roleplaying urge, not a mechanical modifier. Acting on it can earn a special boon during a long rest. Example: Parrien's isolated farm life left him without friends his own age, and he now struggles to make friends. After an in-game day in which he attempts to make a new friend, the player can earn a boon during a long rest.
